Ruxolitinib (Jakavi) Commercial Recip-e Block

When a patient is enrolled in HEM-iSmart, Ruxolitinib is supplied free of charge by the trial sponsor. Selecting commercial Jakavi (CNK 2948107) triggers a hard lock preventing a €3,200+ billing error and offers a 1-click switch to Hospital Trial Pharmacy IMP stock (IMP-HEMISMRT-RUX5).

Case 2 • Belgian Reimbursement & PK Intelligence RIZIV §4460100

Posaconazole: Oncology Convention vs. Crushed 100 mg Tablet

Warns prescribers that Noxafil 40 mg/mL Oral Suspension is only reimbursed via the Belgian Pediatric Oncology Convention (National Tumor Board) and loses 32% absorption with Omeprazole. Recommends switching to Posaconazole 100 mg Gastro-Resistant Tablets (reimbursed via standard RIZIV Chapter IV Bijlage A) which, per UZ Gent PHO TDM data, can be crushed in apple purée to achieve therapeutic trough levels (>0.7 mg/L).

Belgian UHMEP v1.4 Implementation Guide

Multi-Role FHIR R4 Prescription & Dispensing Lifecycle

Every action in PediDose sends live FHIR R4 requests to mock-api-server/server.py with X-Role, Accept-Be-Pseudo: urn:be:fgov:ehealth:pseudo:v1, and Correlation-Id headers.

STEP 1 • ASSISTANT X-Role: assistant
Draft Prescription

POST /ServiceRequest creates a draft order and automatically spawns a linked Task in requested state.

ROLE GUARDRAIL HTTP 403
Assistant $approve Blocked

If X-Role: assistant calls POST /ServiceRequest/{id}/$approve, server.py rejects it with a FHIR OperationOutcome (INSUFFICIENT_ROLE_PRIVILEGES).

STEP 2 • SUPERVISOR X-Role: supervisor
Supervisor Co-Sign ($approve)

POST /ServiceRequest/{id}/$approve transitions status to active and appends the Belgian be-ext-approval extension.

STEP 3 • PHARMACIST X-Role: pharmacist
Task Dispensing & $graph

PATCH /Task/{id} (in-progress → completed) automatically transitions the parent ServiceRequest to completed.

Closed-Loop Clinical Intelligence

AI Traffic-Light Screener & Pharmacist Moderation Gate

  • RED (Blocked): Flags grapefruit juice / pompelmoes submissions due to irreversible intestinal CYP3A4 inhibition (causes severe Vincristine/Ruxolitinib/Tacrolimus toxicity).
  • AMBER (Caution): Flags warm milk with Losec MUPS (alkaline pH strips enteric pellets) or cow milk xanthine oxidase degradation of 6-Mercaptopurine.
  • GREEN (Approved Gold Standard): Pharmacists review and promote safe tips (e.g., crushed Posaconazole 100 mg tablet in 1 tsp cold apple purée, water-ice popsicle before Prednisolone).
